A baby book that doesn't feel like homework.

Paper baby books ask you to remember things later. Momora catches them while they happen, in whatever form you've got.

  1. Save it in ten seconds

    A photo, a video, a sentence you say out loud, their voice, a drawing. It lands on your family's timeline with the date and who was there.

  2. Let the family add theirs

    Your partner adds the bath-time video. Grandma reacts the same day. Everyone sees one timeline, privately.

  3. Print it when you're ready

    Momora picks the best moments of their first year, or any stretch you like, and lays out a printed hardcover book.
