Done before the kids are in bed.

No templates, no design tool, no digging through four thousand photos.

  1. Start your card

    Open Keepsakes in the app, tap the holiday card and choose your greeting. Momora looks back over everything your family saved this year.

  2. Pick the photo, choose a letter

    It suggests your best family photos for the front and drafts three letters about your year: classic, warm and playful. Pick one and change anything you like.

  3. Order your packs

    Cards come in packs of 10 and are mailed to you, ready to address. You get a notification when your card is ready to edit, usually within about 20 minutes.

Point a phone at the card. Watch the year.

The code in the corner opens a short film of your family's year: the swimming lessons, the box boat, the spoon on the floor, with your kids' real voices. Grandma doesn't need an app. She just points her camera.

  • Made from your own photos, clips and recordings
  • Plays in any phone browser, nothing to download
  • Only people with your card can find it

our year, 2026

The letter you never get round to writing.

Momora writes it from the moments you saved, so it sounds like your year rather than a template: the real firsts, the funny bits, the names spelled right. You stay in charge of every word.

Saving more during the year makes a better card in December. That's the only homework.

Good to know

Size and paper
5×7 printed cards, your photo on the front and your letter on the back.
How they come
Packs of 10, mailed to one address in the US or Canada.
Who can order
The family's owner or a manager, with a Momora Plus subscription. One card design per family each year.
Where you order
Inside the Momora app, on iPhone or Android. Prices are shown before you pay.
