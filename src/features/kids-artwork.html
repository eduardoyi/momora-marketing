“Our house”Maya, 4 years · crayon

A minute at the kitchen counter.

  1. Scan

    Tap scan artwork and point your camera at the drawing. Your phone finds the edges and flattens it. Do up to 10 in a row.

  2. Tag the artist

    Pick who made it and give it a title, or say one out loud. “Mama, with flowers” is plenty.

  3. It's on the wall

    Each piece swings onto its own nail, framed, with a placard: their name, their age, what they used. Then the paper can go.

The fridge, but forever.

Every piece hangs in a hand-drawn frame: scalloped, gingham, ric-rac, bobble. No two look the same, so a year of drawings feels like a little gallery, not a camera roll. Go on, nudge one.

Watch their drawings grow up with them.

Because every piece is saved with the artist's age, you can scroll from the first purple scribble to the rocket with handprint stars. Their art sits right next to the photos and stories from the same week, so you remember why they drew it.
