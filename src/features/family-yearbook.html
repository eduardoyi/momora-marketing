a tradition that doesn't need a free weekend

A family yearbook, every year, without the yearly scramble.

Save the moments of the year as they happen. When the year ends, Momora picks the best of it and lays out a hardcover book. Do it every year and the shelf tells the whole story.

How the yearbook happens.

  1. Through the year

    Save a photo, a video or a sentence when something happens. Your partner and the grandparents add theirs too.

  2. At the end of it

    Choose the year. Momora reads everything saved, keeps the best moments and lays out the pages: photos, words, quotes and their drawings.

  3. On the shelf

    Look it over, order from the app, and add it next to last year's.

Each year has its own voice.

A yearbook of photos shows how they looked. A Momora yearbook also keeps what they said that year, what they drew, and codes that play their voice. Year Two sounds different from Year Four, and you'll be glad you can hear both.
