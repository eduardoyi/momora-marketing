More than photos on a page.

A camera roll only remembers what things looked like. A Momora book has the rest of it.

  • The words you wrote at the time, so the photo has its story
  • Their best quotes, set big
  • Codes that play their voice and videos from the printed page
  • Their drawings, scanned and framed
  • Illustrations of your kids, if you use them
