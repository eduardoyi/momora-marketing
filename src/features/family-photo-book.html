A book with everyone's view of the year.

  1. Invite the family

    Send a private code to your partner, the grandparents, the aunt who's always filming. You choose who can add and who can just watch.

  2. Everyone saves as they go

    Photos, videos, a line about what happened. It all lands on one timeline, in order, with who was there.

  3. Momora makes the book

    Pick a span and it chooses the best moments from everyone's photos and lays out a hardcover book.

Photos with the story attached.

A shared album collects photos. Momora also keeps what people said about them: Grandma's note about the picnic, your partner's line about the box boat. Those words go into the book next to the picture.
