We've been burned by these apps too.

The baby book that turned into homework. The app that held the photos hostage. So, three promises, in writing:

  • It never punishes you for missing a week.

    Skip a week, skip a month. No streaks, no guilt, nothing piling up.

  • Your memories are never held hostage.

    Export everything, any time: every photo, video, recording and word.

  • Private by default.

    Only the family you invite can see it. Nothing is ever public.
