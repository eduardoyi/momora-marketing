Tinybeans is a good private family journal for parents who post often and want grandparents to follow along by app, website or email. The free plan allows 20 uploads a month and 5 GB of storage, with ads; Tinybeans+ costs $7.99 a month or $74.99 a year in the US and removes the cap and the ads. The trade-offs are an ad-funded business that uses your data for targeted advertising, and full export only while you pay.

Every Tinybeans fact below was checked on Tinybeans' own website, help center and US App Store listing on November 19, 2026.

Tinybeans runs on iPhone and Android, and on the web. The App Store lists 4.9 stars from about 104,000 ratings as of November 2026.

## How much Tinybeans costs, and when the free plan is enough

Relatives who follow your journal don't need a subscription. The price question is about you, the parent who uploads.

Tinybeans' FAQ lists Tinybeans+ at $74.99 a year or $7.99 a month, charged in US dollars everywhere except Australia, where it's charged in Australian dollars. At $74.99 a year it works out to about $6.25 a month. The App Store offers a [free two-week trial](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tinybeans-private-family-album/id521633042) of Tinybeans+.

The App Store also lists "Tinybeans+ Legacy" at $4.49 and $39.99. Those are older plans: Tinybeans' FAQ says legacy subscriptions stay active while they keep auto-renewing on the original payment method, so new subscribers pay the standard price.

When is free enough? If you post a handful of photos a month for two grandparents, 20 uploads may cover it. If you film a lot, you'll hit 20 by mid-month in a busy stretch, and the [5 GB free storage limit](https://tinybeans.helpscoutdocs.com/article/77-what-happens-when-i-reach-the-5gb-storage-limit-on-the-free-plan) matters too: once a free journal passes 5 GB, Tinybeans blocks new uploads and archives older memories until you upgrade. Video fills 5 GB much faster than photos.

## What grandparents see

A Tinybeans journal is invite-only. On the website you invite relatives by email; in the app you can show a QR code or [send a one-time invite link](https://tinybeans.helpscoutdocs.com/article/73-how-do-i-invite-my-family-and-friends-to-tinybeans) by text, WhatsApp or email. Those links work once and expire after 24 hours, so if Grandma opens hers on day two you'll need to send a new one.

Once she's in, she gets her own login and can watch in the app or on the website. Tinybeans' FAQ says "a smartphone is not required," and relatives who skip both can get updates by email. Tinybeans' homepage says followers can [comment and react from the app or from email updates](https://tinybeans.com/).

![A grandmother at a kitchen table watches a toddler video on her laptop, with an illustrated email card showing the video and a heart button.](https://pub-645d6d0081d7424baba0ca52ae362e9d.r2.dev/images/a191c1f2-db15-4791-b32d-a7c1400218ab/5ea5e5ef-d642-4c9a-a4f8-826bb96f0620/final-1792487023828.webp)

The email digests are the part grandparents tend to love, and the part that can annoy. New followers are signed up for daily update emails automatically. Tinybeans' help center says followers get [a daily update on days you post, plus flashbacks and weekly summaries](https://tinybeans.helpscoutdocs.com/article/15-what-can-my-followers-see-on-tinybeans), and they change the frequency by logging in.

You decide how much each follower can do. Tinybeans' [access levels](https://tinybeans.helpscoutdocs.com/article/105-what-are-the-different-access-levels-i-can-grant-followers) let a follower view or add measurements and milestones, add their own photos and videos to your journal, or have Full Access, which makes them a co-owner with your editing permissions except deleting the journal. You can also mark a memory as hidden when you upload it; hidden memories are seen only by you and Full Access followers, and Tinybeans' FAQ says you can't hide or unhide a moment after it's posted.

If you mainly want a shared album for relatives, our guide to [private family photo sharing apps](/blog/private-family-social-media-apps/) compares Tinybeans with FamilyAlbum, iCloud and Google Photos shared albums.

## How Tinybeans works as a baby journal

You can add a [photo, video, quote, story or voice note](https://tinybeans.helpscoutdocs.com/article/75-how-do-i-add-a-new-memory) as a memory (voice notes and bulk uploads are listed as Tinybeans+ features). Tinybeans tries to detect the date each photo or video was taken and files it on that day in a calendar view, which answers "when was that?" without scrolling a camera roll. Its help center warns that media transferred from other devices can carry the wrong date, so check dates on anything you saved from a messaging app.

![A parent's phone shows a grid of toddler photos with a drawn note card above one of them, while the toddler plays at a coffee table.](https://pub-645d6d0081d7424baba0ca52ae362e9d.r2.dev/images/a191c1f2-db15-4791-b32d-a7c1400218ab/61e4c758-d9b3-42c5-9a5c-98674a3a4061/final-1792487011049.webp "A few lines of caption are what explain a photo years from now.")

Captions are where the journal part happens. "First steps, Grandpa's living room, Aunt Jess cheering" gives you context now and something to remember later. Tinybeans also sells [printed photo books](https://tinybeans.com/photobooks/), with free US shipping for Tinybeans+ subscribers.

## Is Tinybeans safe? Privacy and how it makes money

For who sees your photos, yes: Tinybeans' FAQ says your photos and videos "can only be viewed by the family and friends you invite." A stranger can't find your journal by searching. That control stops at the invitation, though. Anyone you invite can download single photos from the three-dot menu unless you [turn off follower downloads](https://tinybeans.helpscoutdocs.com/article/59-how-do-i-save-download-photos-from-a-journal-i-follow), and nothing stops a screenshot.

What Tinybeans does with your data is a separate question, and its own documents answer it:

- **Advertising pays most of the bills.** In a [2022 Google case study](https://www.google.com/ads/publisher/stories/tinybeans/), Tinybeans' revenue chief said the free, ad-supported service made up about 85% of revenue, with the rest from subscriptions. Tinybeans also sells photo books.
- **"Never sell" has a California-law footnote.** Tinybeans' [About page](https://tinybeans.com/about/) says "we will never sell your data or personal information." Its [privacy policy](https://tinybeans.com/privacy) (last updated April 2, 2024) says it won't sell, share or trade your information to unrelated third parties for their own marketing, but also that it may "sell" and/or "share" personal information with advertising partners for targeted advertising, which can count as a sale or share under California law even though Tinybeans isn't paid for the data. It names Freestar as the partner that places ads and says Freestar collects data for advertising. The policy explains how to opt out.
- **The App Store privacy label is broad.** Tinybeans' [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tinybeans-private-family-album/id521633042) says location, contact info, user content, identifiers and usage data may be used to track you across other companies' apps and websites. Apple notes the label is the developer's own and isn't verified by Apple.
- **Your content is analyzed.** The privacy policy says Tinybeans may use automated processes and machine learning to analyze user content to spot trends and build features. It also says you own your photos and videos.
- **Security is described in general terms.** The policy promises "commercially reasonable" security procedures and says no online transmission can be guaranteed.

So is Tinybeans safe? It's a legitimate company, founded in Australia in 2012 and listed on the Australian Securities Exchange since 2017, according to [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tinybeans), and the journal itself is private. The free experience is paid for by advertising tied to your data.

## Cancelling Tinybeans: what happens to your memories

To cancel in the app, tap More, then Manage Subscription. If you subscribed on the website, Tinybeans' help center says you [cancel from the billing page on the website](https://tinybeans.helpscoutdocs.com/article/26-explore-can-i-cancel-my-subscription) instead. Paid features continue until the end of your current term.

Cancelling is where subscription apps tend to surprise people, so here is what Tinybeans' own help center says happens:

- Your memories aren't deleted. Tinybeans says "we'll never delete your memories just because you cancel."
- But your journal drops to the 5 GB free limit. If it holds more than that, your [newest memories within 5 GB stay visible and older ones are archived](https://tinybeans.helpscoutdocs.com/article/87-will-my-memories-be-deleted-if-i-cancel-my-subscription-or-downgrade), no longer visible in the app, and can be accessed again if you upgrade. Uploads stop until you're back under the limit or paying.
- Bulk export is a Tinybeans+ feature, so once you cancel, you lose the easy way to get everything out.
- The same help page warns that if your account is inactive for 12 months, "your data may be permanently deleted."

None of that is hidden, but it is spread across several help articles. A parent with years of video on Tinybeans+ who cancels without exporting first will find the oldest memories locked behind a resubscription. The fair reading: Tinybeans keeps your memories, but access beyond 5 GB and bulk export are things you pay for.

## How to export your Tinybeans memories

Anyone can save a single photo or video from a memory's three-dot menu. For everything, [bulk export](https://tinybeans.helpscoutdocs.com/article/28-how-can-i-export-all-of-my-moments) works like this:

1. You need Tinybeans+, and you must be the journal owner or a follower with Full Access.
2. Log in at tinybeans.com, click your name at the top right and choose "journal details." The export option is at the bottom.
3. Pick a date range. Tinybeans recommends no more than three years per request so the export doesn't fail.
4. Tinybeans emails download links for your photos and videos, one month at a time. The links expire after five days, and they may not work with an ad blocker on.

Tinybeans doesn't say whether captions, dates or comments come out with the files, or what file format and quality you get. Captions do carry into its photo books. If Tinybeans is your only baby book, export a few months now, open the files, and check what came with them. Do the full export before you cancel, not after.

To delete your account, use the More tab in the app or email Tinybeans. The privacy policy says personal information is kept as long as needed for the purposes it was collected for, without a specific timeline.

## Who Tinybeans fits, and who should look elsewhere

Tinybeans fits you if:

- You post updates often and want grandparents to follow by email or browser without learning an app.
- You want a dated, calendar-style diary with milestones and optional printed photo books.
- You're comfortable with ads and ad-related data use on the free plan, or happy to pay $74.99 a year.

Look elsewhere if:

- You want free, uncapped uploads. Our [Tinybeans vs FamilyAlbum comparison](/blog/tinybeans-vs-family-album/) covers the main free alternative.
- Ad-funded data practices are a deal-breaker for a journal full of your children.
- You need a documented export that keeps captions and dates with each photo.

## FAQ

### Is Tinybeans free?

Yes, with limits. The free plan allows 20 photo or video uploads a month and 5 GB of storage, with ads. Relatives who follow your journal don't pay.

### How much does Tinybeans cost?

Tinybeans+ is $7.99 a month or $74.99 a year in the US as of November 2026, with a two-week free trial on the App Store. It adds unlimited uploads, 200 GB, five-minute videos, no ads, a second account and bulk export.

### Is Tinybeans safe?

Your journal is private: only people you invite can see it. Tinybeans is ad-supported, though, and its privacy policy says it may share personal information with advertising partners for targeted advertising. Its App Store label lists location, identifiers, usage data and user content as data that may be used to track you.

### How does Tinybeans make money?

Mostly from advertising. In a 2022 Google case study, Tinybeans said its free, ad-supported service made up about 85% of revenue, with the rest from Tinybeans+ subscriptions. It also sells printed photo books.

### What happens to my photos if I cancel Tinybeans+?

They aren't deleted, but your journal falls to the 5 GB free limit. Older memories beyond 5 GB are archived and hidden until you upgrade, and bulk export stops being available, so export before you cancel.
