## FamilyAlbum app review: the short verdict

FamilyAlbum is a good free choice if your main goal is getting baby photos and videos to grandparents privately. The free plan gives you unlimited storage in an invite-only album, and one paid subscription covers everyone in it. The trade-offs: free videos are capped at two minutes, free albums show third-party ads, uploads are slightly resized, and saving photos in bulk needs the top Premium Family Pro plan ($10.99 a month).

Everything below was checked on FamilyAlbum's own Premium page, help center, privacy page and app store listings in November 2026. A disclosure: we make Momora, a competing family-memory app, so read our take with that in mind.

FamilyAlbum is made by MIXI, Inc., a Japanese company. As of November 2026, the [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/familyalbum-share-baby-photos/id935672069) shows 4.9 stars from about 383,000 ratings, and [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=us.mitene) shows 4.8 stars from about 369,000 reviews, 10 million-plus downloads and a "Contains ads" label. The iPhone app now requires iOS 18 or later.

## How FamilyAlbum works

One parent creates the album and becomes an Album Admin. Admins [manage invitations, member accounts, visibility and settings](https://help.family-album.com/hc/en-us/articles/4404247061529-What-s-the-difference-between-Album-Admins-and-Album-Members); everyone else joins as an Album Member with fewer controls. An album can hold [up to 100 members](https://help.family-album.com/hc/en-us/articles/360038267214-What-is-FamilyAlbum).

To invite relatives, an Admin goes to [Settings, then Invite Family](https://help.family-album.com/hc/en-us/articles/360038113854-How-do-I-invite-family-to-my-album) and sends either an app invitation or a browser invitation. If Grandpa is sitting next to you, he can [scan a QR code](https://help.family-album.com/hc/en-us/articles/360052306734-How-do-I-use-a-QR-code-to-invite-someone-to-my-album) on your screen.

Uploads are sorted by month with your child's age on each batch, so you never make folders. Admins can share a post with the whole album or keep it between Admins, which is handy for the blurry shots you're not ready to show anyone.

What relatives can do depends on how they joined. FamilyAlbum says [app users can view by month, favorite and upload, while browser users can view, save and comment](https://help.family-album.com/hc/en-us/articles/360038640593-What-s-the-difference-between-the-app-version-and-browser-version-of-FamilyAlbum) in one long chronological scroll. Parent (Admin) accounts are limited to the app. Admins also decide whether members can comment or download at all.

FamilyAlbum is a photo feed with comments. It isn't a milestone journal or a designed baby book. Grandma can write "Look at those cheeks!" under a photo, but there's no dedicated place for the first word or the story behind the picture on the free plan. Premium Family adds Personal Pages with milestones and monthly journal entries, written by Admins only.

### Which limits you'll actually hit

The two-minute video cap comes first. Some summaries online say three minutes; FamilyAlbum's own help center says two minutes free and ten with Premium Family or Pro. Bath clips and first steps fit. A full preschool song, a birthday candle sequence or a bedtime story often runs longer. FamilyAlbum says videos over two minutes stay viewable if you later cancel Premium.

The computer upload limit matters if you shoot on a "real" camera or keep years of photos on a laptop. If everything starts on your phone, you may never notice it. When you do upload from a computer, FamilyAlbum's limits are [200 MB per image and 5 GB per video](https://help.family-album.com/hc/en-us/articles/360038720993-What-restrictions-are-there-on-uploading-photos-and-videos-Also-is-there-a-limit-to-the-amount-of-photos-and-videos-I-can-upload); sharing from another iOS app tops out at 120 MB, and HEVC files aren't supported.

Bulk downloads are the Pro feature to weigh most. Saving one photo at a time is fine for a weekend. Saving a whole year that way is a slow evening (see the download section below).

### Ads

Free albums show third-party ads. FamilyAlbum says [any Premium plan removes third-party ads](https://help.family-album.com/hc/en-us/articles/45910504655513-I-want-to-hide-ads), but its own banners, such as promotions for FamilyAlbum products, can't be hidden. Its store listings also advertise free monthly photo prints, so expect print offers in the app.

### Trial and billing

[First-time subscribers get a one-month free trial](https://family-album.com/premium) of any plan. It renews and charges automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends, and an account that has had Premium before isn't eligible again. Uninstalling the app or deleting your account [doesn't cancel the subscription](https://help.family-album.com/hc/en-us/articles/360038781113-How-do-I-cancel-my-Premium-subscription): cancel in the App Store, Google Play or the FamilyAlbum web portal. Premium stays active until the end of the period you paid for, and canceling a Family plan doesn't delete any photos; the album drops back to the free version.

## What FamilyAlbum does well

1. **One private place for every relative.** Invite-only albums with up to 100 members cover both sets of grandparents, the aunt in Denver and the cousins. Photos go up once and nobody gets left off a text thread.
2. **The timeline organizes itself.** Uploads are sorted by month and labeled with your child's age. When Grandma asks what the baby looked like at eight months, she scrolls to eight months.
3. **Grandparents don't need to install anything.** The browser invitation lets less app-comfortable relatives view, comment and save from a link.
4. **The free plan stands on its own.** Unlimited storage, comments, favorites and a compilation video every three months. A family that shares phone photos can use it for years without paying.
5. **One subscription covers the album.** On Premium Family or Pro, [every member gets the features](https://help.family-album.com/hc/en-us/articles/360038264494-What-is-FamilyAlbum-Premium), so grandparents don't pay separately.

## Where FamilyAlbum falls short

1. **Two-minute free videos.** The limit most likely to push you toward paying if you film recitals or whole birthday songs.
2. **Ads and promotions on the free plan.** Third-party ads go away with Premium; FamilyAlbum's own product banners don't.
3. **Resized files.** FamilyAlbum says [uploads are slightly resized](https://help.family-album.com/hc/en-us/articles/4404482216601-Do-you-resize-uploads) to save data and server costs, and downloads and DVDs contain the resized versions. Keep your originals somewhere else.
4. **Batched notifications.** Upload several items within five minutes and relatives get [one notification, which can take up to 10 minutes](https://help.family-album.com/hc/en-us/articles/360054997774-I-m-not-receiving-any-push-notifications-What-should-I-do). If someone opens the post in Recents first, no alert is sent. "Did you see the photos?" will sometimes get "What photos?"
5. **Reactions are a paid add-on.** FamilyAlbum's sticker [reactions need a sticker subscription](https://help.family-album.com/hc/en-us/articles/40886056781721-Reaction-feature), and they don't send notifications. Ask relatives to comment if they want you to notice.
6. **Background uploads cost extra.** Uploads that keep going while you use other apps are a Pro feature, on iOS only, so on other plans big batches go best on stable Wi-Fi with the app open.
7. **Little room for stories on the free plan.** It's a photo feed. If what you want in ten years is your toddler's mispronounced words or the story behind a photo, think about whether a feed will hold that.

## How to download your photos from FamilyAlbum

**One photo or video (any plan):** in the app, open the Album tab, tap the photo or video, tap the menu in the top-right corner, then tap "Download". In the browser version, click the photo and then the "Download" button. That's FamilyAlbum's [own step-by-step](https://help.family-album.com/hc/en-us/articles/360038259334-How-do-I-save-photos-videos). On iPhone the file lands in the Photos app; on Android, in the Download folder.

**Many at once (Premium Family Pro):** open the Album tab, tap the menu in the top-right corner and choose "Bulk Download". This works only in the app, and iPhones are limited to [100 items per download](https://help.family-album.com/hc/en-us/articles/360038776533-Can-I-save-or-delete-multiple-photos-videos-at-once). Four years of baby photos means a lot of batches.

**Everything for a period, with comments:** order a [PC-version FamilyAlbum DVD](https://help.family-album.com/hc/en-us/articles/360038780153-What-are-FamilyAlbum-DVDs). It holds all photos and videos for any timespan and includes a viewer that shows comments. It costs $40.99 plus $3.99 shipping (free with Premium Family or Pro), extra discs are $12.99 each if your selection doesn't fit, and delivery takes about two to three weeks.

A few things to know before you rely on any of these:

- **Downloads need permission.** If an Admin set [Download Permission to "Admins Only"](https://help.family-album.com/hc/en-us/articles/360038259714-Can-grandparents-and-other-relatives-save-photos-and-videos-and-delete-comments), members can't save anything.
- **Dates mostly carry over.** Saved files usually keep FamilyAlbum's date taken; if there isn't one, the download date is used, which can scramble a camera roll.
- **You get the resized versions,** not your originals, from both downloads and DVDs.
- **There's no one-tap export** of a whole album to a computer.

### Leaving FamilyAlbum

Leaving is several separate steps. Cancel Premium in the store you paid through. Save what you want to keep. Then delete your account under [Settings, then Inquiries, then Delete Account](https://help.family-album.com/hc/en-us/articles/360038529013-How-do-I-delete-my-account); deleting a whole album goes through a support request on the same screen. FamilyAlbum's privacy page says [deleted photos and closed accounts are removed from its servers within 30 days](https://family-album.com/privacy). Uninstalling the app does none of this.

Before you build a multi-year archive, save one month of photos and one short video to your phone and check the dates and quality. Our [photo organization guide](/blog/photo-organization-programs/) explains how a shared album differs from a real backup.

## Is FamilyAlbum private and safe?

Albums are invite-only, with no public feed or search. FamilyAlbum's privacy page says uploads [can only be viewed by you and the people you invite](https://family-album.com/privacy), that data is encrypted in transit, and that it doesn't sell your data to third parties. That's encryption in transit, not end-to-end encryption. The same page says it collects device and usage data and uses it for marketing analytics, and the App Store privacy label lists identifiers as data that may be used to track you across other companies' apps and websites.

![Hands hold a phone beneath two drawn doors, one marked with a key and settings gear and one with heart and comment symbols, on a table with family photos.](https://pub-645d6d0081d7424baba0ca52ae362e9d.r2.dev/images/8a94ea08-6c83-48ec-afe9-6c910273d82c/76ac394f-570e-41a6-a8ba-4c1e83c62833/final-1792488552680.webp "Member access gives grandparents the feed and comments; keep invitations and settings with the other parent.")

Invites limit who sees a photo, not what they do with it: anyone you invite can save or screenshot it. Make grandparents Members, not Admins, and turn off downloads if you'd rather they didn't save. ## Which families FamilyAlbum suits

Start free if you mainly want a private feed for grandparents, your clips are short and everyone shoots on phones. Pay for Premium Family ($59 a year) if you regularly film longer than two minutes, upload from a computer or want ads gone for everyone in the album. Pay for Pro ($109 a year) mainly if you want bulk downloads, full-HD video or TV casting.

Look at a different kind of app if you want a personal, book-like record more than a shared feed, or a portable archive of your original files. ## FAQ

### Is FamilyAlbum free?

Yes. The free plan includes unlimited photo and video storage and uploads, comments, favorites and a compilation video every three months. Free videos are limited to two minutes, and free albums show third-party ads.

### How much is FamilyAlbum Premium?

As of November 2026 in the US: Premium One is $2.99 a month (subscriber only), Premium Family is $5.99 a month or $59 a year, and Premium Family Pro is $10.99 a month or $109 a year. Family plans cover everyone in the album, and first-time subscribers get one month free.

### How long can videos be on FamilyAlbum?

Up to two minutes on the free plan and Premium One, and up to ten minutes on Premium Family and Premium Family Pro.

### How do I download pictures from the FamilyAlbum app?

Tap the photo, tap the menu in the top-right corner and choose "Download". To save many at once you need Premium Family Pro ("Bulk Download" in the Album tab menu, up to 100 items at a time on iPhone). A PC-version DVD is the way to get a whole timespan with comments.

### What is the best app to share photos with family?

It depends on the job. For free, private sharing with grandparents who may not install apps, FamilyAlbum is hard to beat. Shared albums in iCloud or Google Photos work if everyone already uses them, and journal-style apps suit parents who want stories and a book.
