## Is Chatbooks worth it? The quick answer

Yes, if you want small printed photo books with very little effort and you'll keep an eye on your subscription. As of November 2026, Chatbooks' cheapest plan, Monthly Minis, costs $10 a book (5x5-inch softcover, 30 to 60 pages) or $100 a year for 12 books, with free standard shipping. One-off custom photo books start at $26.

The catches are in the fine print, not the price: subscription books print automatically if you don't act (Autoship is on by default), unused credits expire, annual plans renew at the current price, and fees are generally non-refundable. Print quality gets good marks from customers; billing and cancellation get most of the complaints.

Everything below was checked on chatbooks.com and its help center on November 19, 2026.

## Chatbooks subscription fine print: Autoship, credits, renewal and cancelling

This is where most Chatbooks complaints start, so it's worth five minutes before you subscribe. Per Chatbooks' [billing help page](https://help.chatbooks.com/en/articles/7906070-how-does-billing-work-for-monthbook-chatbooks-subscriptions):

- **Autoship is on by default.** When a book has 30 pages and a credit is available, Chatbooks notifies you, gives you 7 days to edit, then sends it to print automatically.
- **Credits expire 13 months after purchase.** You can extend a credit in the app within 30 days of its expiration date.
- **Annual plans renew automatically** for another full year at the current annual price, which may be higher than a promotional first year. Chatbooks emails you about 30 days before renewal with the subject "Important Information About Your Subscription," so check your spam or promotions folder.
- **Before renewal you can** switch to monthly billing, pause for one billing cycle (yourself in the iOS app; through Support on Android or the web) or cancel.

Chatbooks' [terms of service](https://chatbooks.com/terms-of-service) say subscriptions renew until you cancel and that fees are non-refundable unless the agreement says otherwise.

### How to cancel Chatbooks

In the app, open your subscription, tap the gear icon, scroll to the bottom and choose cancel. On the web, open the subscription from My Books or Account, click the gear icon and cancel at the bottom. Cancel before your billing date to avoid the next charge. ([Chatbooks' cancellation steps](https://help.chatbooks.com/en/articles/10771286-how-to-cancel-my-monthly-mini-or-monthbooks-subscription).)

What happens to your credits:

- Regular subscription credits stay usable for 30 days after you cancel, on subscription books or other purchases.
- Credits from a promotional offer [expire as soon as you cancel](https://help.chatbooks.com/en/articles/8427706-if-i-cancel-my-subscription-can-i-still-use-my-credits). Support can reactivate the subscription and restore unexpired promo credits.

The old "12 Books for $12" Minis deal has [ended](https://help.chatbooks.com/en/articles/12593568-12-books-for-12-no-longer-available); people who bought it renew at the plan's current price after their 12 credits. Chatbooks also posts rotating discount codes on its [codes page](https://chatbooks.com/chatbooks-code); they change often, so treat any code as temporary.

## Print quality, ease of use and the guarantee

### How the books are made

For the Layflat, Chatbooks lists Pacesetter 80# silk paper, archival ink "certified to resist fading for 200 years," printing up to 600 DPI, PUR perfect binding, made in the USA. Monthly Minis use the same archival ink, 600 DPI printing and PUR binding, with "kid-proof rounded edges". Those specs describe the materials; how a book looks depends mostly on your photos. Phone shots taken in dim light will look dim on paper.

### How much work it takes

Chatbooks is built for tapping, not designing. Custom Photo Books can auto-select your best photos and skip blurry shots, duplicates and screenshots, and you can filter by faces and dates. Photos can come from your camera roll, Google Photos or a computer. For a monthly book you still:

- Pick 30 to 60 photos (or let the filter narrow them)
- Write any captions you want kept
- Check the book in the 7-day Autoship window, or it prints as is

Budget around 15 minutes a month. If the bigger job is sorting thousands of photos first, see our [photo organization guide](/blog/photo-organization-programs/).

### Where it falls short

Design control is limited on purpose. You get layouts, collages, captions and a cover title, not page-by-page freedom. Sizes top out at 10x10, so there's no 11x14 or 12x12 coffee-table option.

### The guarantee and returns

Under the [Chatbooks guarantee](https://help.chatbooks.com/en/articles/8063204-what-is-the-chatbooks-guarantee), if a book's printing, shipping, feel or smell is wrong, including damage on arrival, Chatbooks says it will "make it right." "Toddler Insurance" replaces a book a young child has damaged, once per book, within one year of the order date, with a photo of the damage. The [return policy](https://chatbooks.com/return-policy) lists free prepaid return labels, no restocking fee, refunds within 10 business days of receiving the item, and a 30-day window to report print, binding or shipping problems.

## What Chatbooks customers say

On [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/chatbooks.com), Chatbooks had a TrustScore of 3.2 out of 5 from 2,229 reviews when we checked on November 19, 2026, with 69% five-star and 11% one-star ratings. Trustpilot notes the company hasn't replied to negative reviews. Trustpilot's review summary groups the praise around ease of use and the printed books themselves, and the complaints around mid-subscription price increases, expiring credits, hard-to-find cancellation and charges after cancelling, slow support and late deliveries.

That pattern matches the fine print above: the product is the easy part, the subscription needs watching. Set a calendar reminder for your renewal date and your credit expiration dates.

## Chatbooks vs. Shutterfly and other options

Shutterfly gives you far more design control. Its [Create Your Own photo book](https://www.shutterfly.com/p/photo-books/photo-book-styles/create-your-own-photo-book) offers fully customizable layouts plus stickers, backgrounds, embellishments and text, along with auto-layout. [Sizes run from 8x8 to 12x12](https://www.shutterfly.com/photo-books), and books go up to 111 pages. On November 19, 2026, the Create Your Own page listed a 20-page book at $47.99 regular ($28.79 on sale) and extra pages at $2.74 regular ($1.92 on sale). Shutterfly runs sales constantly, so compare regular prices.

Is Shutterfly or Chatbooks better? Chatbooks is better if you want a small book done from your phone in minutes, with free shipping and a subscription that keeps books coming. Shutterfly is better if you want to design every page or need a bigger book.

Once Upon sits in between: it [lays out photos for you](https://onceupon.photo/en-us/how-it-works) and lets you add longer captions. As of November 2026, its [US prices](https://onceupon.photo/en-us/photobooks) start at $21 for an 8x8 softcover, $25 for an 8x8 hardcover and $37 for an 11x11 hardcover (20 pages), with flat $5 shipping and 15 to 20 business days for US delivery.

## FAQ

### How much is Chatbooks per month?

As of November 2026, $10 a month for Monthly Minis (5x5 softcover) and $15 to $34 a month for Monthbooks, depending on size and cover. Paying annually gets 12 books for the price of 10, for example $100 a year for Minis. Standard US shipping is free.

### How much are Chatbooks after the first month?

The same regular price, unless you started on a promotion. Promotional first periods (like the discontinued 12 Books for $12 deal) renew at the plan's current price, and annual plans renew at the current annual price.

### Is Chatbooks a legitimate company?

Yes. Chatbooks, Inc. is based in Lehi, Utah, and was founded in 2014 by Vanessa and Nate Quigley, according to the company's [founder interview](https://chatbooks.com/blog/vanessa-quigley-founder-interview) and [terms of service](https://chatbooks.com/terms-of-service). Complaints on Trustpilot are mostly about subscription billing, expiring credits and support, not about scams or books never arriving.

### Is Shutterfly or Chatbooks better?

Chatbooks for quick, small books from your phone and hands-off monthly subscriptions. Shutterfly for page-by-page design, more embellishments and larger sizes up to 12x12.

### Who does the best quality photo books?

It depends on what "quality" means to you. Chatbooks' Layflat (80# silk paper, archival ink, flat-opening pages) is its premium option, but it tops out at 10x10. For heavier paper, bigger sizes and heirloom covers, premium printers such as Artifact Uprising and Printique, compared in our [Chatbooks alternatives guide](/blog/chatbooks-alternatives/), are worth a look. Whichever you choose, sharp, well-lit original photos matter more than the printer.

### Does Chatbooks sell your data?

Chatbooks' [privacy policy](https://chatbooks.com/privacy-policy) says some disclosures to advertising partners "may broadly be considered a 'sale'" of personal information under state privacy laws, and that it does not sell sensitive personal information. You can opt out of targeted advertising, and Chatbooks treats a browser's Global Privacy Control signal as an opt-out. To delete your data, email privacy@chatbooks.com from your account address; Chatbooks may ask for information to verify your identity.
